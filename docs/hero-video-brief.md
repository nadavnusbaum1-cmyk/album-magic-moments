# HeyMori — Hero Video Brief

A short, looping video for the top of the HeyMori landing page (heymori.co.il).
Hand this to a videographer, motion designer, or an AI video tool (Runway, Pika,
Kling, Sora, Higgsfield, Veo, etc.).

## The product (context)
**HeyMori** is an AI photo companion for events. Guests scan a QR code, send a
selfie, and **Mori** (the friendly AI) instantly finds every photo they appear in
— plus guests add their own photos. Feeling: joyful, personal, effortless, warm.
Tagline: *"HeyMori — your AI photo buddy."*

## What we need
A **10–20 second seamless loop** that sits in a rounded 16:9 frame in the hero,
**autoplaying, muted, on repeat**. All headline text lives in the web page over/around
it — so the **video itself should have NO baked-in text** (or leave generous empty
space if any). It reads as "look how magical and easy this is."

## Format & specs (must-have)
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (landscape)
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (1280×720 acceptable)
- **Length:** 10–20s, **loops seamlessly** (first & last frame match)
- **Audio:** none needed (plays muted)
- **File:** MP4 (H.264), **under ~8 MB** (compress; it loads on mobile)
- **Also deliver:** a still poster frame as `hero-poster.jpg`
- **Safe & clean:** no on-screen text, no logos other than optional subtle HeyMori mark

## Brand look
- **Palette:** soft lavender / purple (#7C5CD3), deep navy (#2B2540), warm pink & peach accents, lots of light/white space
- **Mood:** bright, celebratory, premium-but-friendly — *not* corporate, *not* childish
- **Font (if any text/UI shown):** Nunito (rounded)
- **Character (optional):** "Mori" — a friendly 3D-cartoon girl in a purple hoodie with an "M". Can cameo, but don't force her in.

## Two directions (pick one)

### Option A — Product demo (recommended, most convincing)
A clean screen-capture style flow of the actual experience:
1. A phone scans a **QR code** at an event.
2. The HeyMori page opens; guest **takes a selfie** (front camera).
3. A friendly **"Mori is looking for you…"** searching state (sparkle/scan animation).
4. A **grid of the guest's photos appears** — faces matching, photos popping in.
5. Guest taps **download / share**. End on the full happy gallery.
*Tone: fast, satisfying, "wow that was instant."* Best produced as a real screen
recording of the live app + light motion polish (no AI needed).

### Option B — Atmospheric brand montage (AI-friendly)
Short, warm, candid event moments intercut with the "magic" idea:
1. Golden-hour **wedding/party candids** — laughter, dancing, hugs (diverse, real-feeling).
2. A guest holds up a phone, **takes a selfie**.
3. Soft lavender **particles / sparkles** sweep across — the "AI finds you" beat.
4. **Photos float/tile into a gallery** on a phone screen.
5. End on a warm smile / group moment.
*Tone: emotional, premium, memory-focused. No UI accuracy required.*
